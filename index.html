Sobre o Grupo

Dedicado ao estudo do papel da glia em aspectos da fisiopatologia do sistema nervoso, explorando modelos de doenças neurodegenerativas e ação de extratos de plantas regionais, especialmente dos flavonóides, na busca de biomarcadores e alvos terapeuticos; tem significativa atuação na formação de recursos humanos e detém cooperações consolidadas com grupos nacionais e internacionais com indicadores tais como estágios sanduíches de alunos, co-orientações, estágios pós-doutorais e crescente numero de publicações. Insere-se no INCT de Neurociência Translacional (INNT) e no INCT de D. Cerebro Neurotoxicidade e Neuroproteção. Seus pesquisadores participam de um total de 8 diferentes programas de pós-graduação, incluindo o Doutorado de Neurociência (PGNet) do INNT e têm projetos de popularização da ciência em escolas públicas de Salvador. Muitos dos egressos iniciados em ciência e titulados estão inseridos em diversas instituições de ensino/pesquisa

Glioblastomas (GBM) are malignant tumours with poor prognosis. Treatment involves chemotherapy and/or radiotherapy; however, there is currently no standard treatment for recurrence, and prognosis remains unfavourable. Inflammatory mediators and microRNAs (miRNAs) influence the aggressiveness of GBM, being involved in the communication with the cells of the tumour parenchyma, including microglia/macrophages, and maintaining an immunosuppressive microenvironment.

Traumatic brain injury (TBI) has a complex and multifactorial pathology and is a major cause of death and disability for humans. Immediately after TBI, astrocytes and microglia react with complex morphological and functional changes known as reactive gliosis to form a glial scar in the area immediately adjacent to the lesion, which is the major barrier to neuronal regeneration.

Monocrotaline (MCT) has well-characterized hepatotoxic and pneumotoxic effects attributed to its active pyrrole metabolites. Studies have previously shown that astrocytes and neurons are targets of MCT, and that toxicity is attributed to astrocyte P450 metabolism to reactive metabolites. However, little is known about MCT toxicity and metabolism by brain endothelial cells (BECs), cells that, together with astrocytes, are specialized in xenobiotic metabolism and neuroprotection.

The imbalance between the overproduction of reactive species and antioxidant mechanisms can result in astrogliosis and oxidative stress associated with neurodegeneration. Based on the described antioxidant activity of naturally occurring flavonoids, this study evaluated the antioxidant mechanisms of the flavonoid naringenin and the senecioic acid ester derivatives in cortical astrocytes.
